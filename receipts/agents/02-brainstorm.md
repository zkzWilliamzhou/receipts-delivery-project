# 02 Brainstorm agent

**Mission.** Turn "a powerful, useful, novel program that could get 105k stars" into one buildable design, by attacking every candidate.

**Inputs.** `obra/superpowers/skills/brainstorming`, `mattpocock/skills` `grill-me` and `grilling`, the two research reports.

**Outputs.** `docs/BRAINSTORM.md` and `docs/DESIGN.md`.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- Three real approaches, with the rejected ones named and reasons given.
- At least 12 self-grilling questions, including the uncomfortable ones about faking, trust, and stars.
- Goals, non-goals, anti-goals, constraints, and a builder check.
- The user's "work until done" recorded as design approval, since the brainstorming gate otherwise requires it.
- No code written before this document exists.

**Rejection triggers.** A design chosen before alternatives. A star forecast presented as a promise. One-sentence novelty claim with no falsifiable form. Hiding the fact that a malicious agent can bypass the wrapper.
